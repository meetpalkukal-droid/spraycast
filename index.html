How hours are rated

Each hour is checked against every factor below. The worst result sets the hour's color, and the hour's detail always names the factor responsible.

Restricted-label rules allow winds of 3–10 mph and treat anything outside that range, or outside the label time window, as Do not spray. Thresholds marked "Prototype" are placeholders awaiting review by Extension specialists.

Forecast data

Hourly forecasts come from Open-Meteo [8]. For about the first 48 hours they use NOAA's 3 km HRRR model [9], and NOAA's GFS after that. Each pin uses the forecast for the model grid cell that contains it, with no interpolation. Forecasts refresh hourly, and later days are less certain.
